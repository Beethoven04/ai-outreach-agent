### Contacts: one row per person (never one email for two people)
`contact_id, company_no (→ Companies #), company, company_domain, first_name, last_name, full_name, role, country, linkedin_url, public_emails, email, email_source, email_status, email_detail, email_tried, email_attempts, email_checked_at, allow_risky ☑, contact_status, notes`

- `email_status`: *(empty = to find)* → `to_verify` → `valid` | `risky` | `not_found`. Rejected addresses are cleared, remembered in `email_tried`, and the next source is tried.
- `allow_risky` ☑: only tick if you accept a catch-all/unverifiable address.
- `contact_status`: `active | replied | refused | do_not_contact | bounced`. Only `active` people are emailed.
- To give an address yourself: type it in `email` and clear `email_status`. It is verified automatically.

### Messages: one row per email (cold, fu1, fu2)
`msg_id, contact_id, company, first_name, type, parent_msg_id, subject, body, status, approved ☑, qc_score, qc_reasons, qc_model, qc_hash, qc_at, to_email, dry_run_at, test_sent_at, sent_at, gmail_message_id, gmail_thread_id, rfc_message_id, followup_due, reply_at, error, updated_at`

- `body` = greeting + text only (no signature).
- `status`: `draft → needs_fix | ready → sending → sent → replied | bounced`, plus `held` (colleague replied), `cancelled` (follow-up no longer needed), `error`.
- `approved` ☑: nothing is sent without it. Editing subject/body after QC **clears it automatically** and re-runs QC.

### Replies (automatic)
`reply_id, received_at, msg_id, contact_id, full_name, company, from_email, kind (human|auto_reply|bounce), category, summary, next_step, snippet, thread_link, action_taken`

### Log (automatic, append-only)
`ts, workflow, level (info|warn|error), event, msg_id, contact_id, detail`. It is the source of truth for "already sent" (duplicate guard).

### Profile (facts the QC accepts)
`fact_id, fact, source, confirmed`. 20 facts from your CV and portfolio. **F05 (devUP dates) conflicts**: CV says *June 2025 – September 2026*, portfolio says *Present*. Edit F05 to the truth.
